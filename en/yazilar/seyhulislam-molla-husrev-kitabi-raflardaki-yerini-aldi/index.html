The Book on Shaykh al-Islam Molla Hüsrev Is Now on the Shelves

November 14, 2025About 2 min read

Translated from the Turkish original.Read the original

Date of publication of the book: November 2025

In today's Islamic world, the Ottoman scholars are not very well known for their scholarly personalities and views. The aim of our work is to introduce more fully the life, the surroundings and the scholarly identity of Molla Hüsrev (d. 885/1480), who lived in the 15th century and was the great Islamic jurist and statesman of his time. Molla Hüsrev is a scholar who took on many duties in the first periods of the Ottoman golden age (1453-1603). He held influential offices especially during the reigns of Murad II and Sultan Mehmed the Conqueror. He was born in Anatolia, completed his studies there and carried out his first works there. Until that period, the Ottoman scholars used to study the Islamic sciences in Egypt, Aleppo, Baghdad, Damascus and the lands of Persia. Sultan Mehmed the Conqueror, by taking important steps and bringing innovations in scholarly life, turned Istanbul too into a centre of learning.

As a scholar who was trained in Anatolia, Molla Hüsrev became an authority in sciences such as tafsir, fiqh, usul al-fiqh, the etiquette of debate, kalam, logic and rhetoric; he produced original ideas and distinguished himself by his scholarly personality. His works were taught as textbooks in the Ottoman madrasas for centuries. For this reason, within the scholarly tradition of those times, many glosses, commentaries, annotations and abridgements were written on his works.

He served at every level of the Ottoman learned hierarchy, as a madrasa professor, a judge (qadi), a military judge (kazasker) and as Shaykh al-Islam. With his fiqh views and fatwas he made great contributions to the formation of the Ottoman legal system. He took on important duties in the establishment of the madrasas (universities) and in the opening up of learning. With his own fiqh views he also had many debates with the scholars of his time and wrote treatises in reply to them. In this way we will have gained the opportunity to know him as an original man of learning.
