The Book on the Great Imam Abu Hanifa Is Out!

November 14, 2025About 2 min read

Translated from the Turkish original.Read the original

Date of publication of the book: July 2025

The Great Imam Abu Hanifa, Nu'man ibn Thabit (may Allah have mercy on him), is a jurist, mujtahid and man of learning who holds a very important place in the history of Islamic law.

He took very great steps in the science of fiqh, opened a golden age, laid down methods that have been applied for centuries, ensured the development of law in the theoretical sense, brought about the codification of fiqh and left written works to the following generations. In his own age he fulfilled the requirements of Islamic fiqh, and he led the way for Islam to be lived correctly in society as a religion and to give direction to the lives of people and societies. The Greatest Imam (Imam-i A'zam) lived in the periods of both the Umayyad and the Abbasid Caliphates and witnessed the social, political, legal and economic events and problems of both societies.

By examining the events of the century in which he lived very well, he produced Islamic solutions and offered them for the benefit of individuals and societies. He was in contact with every section of society, and in the face of new developments he produced true solutions by ijtihad. Because he had earlier been a merchant, he also, together with his students, made legal arrangements and set out rulings that answered the needs in the economic field.

He stood against the people of innovation (bid'a) and misguidance who sprang up like a fashion in those times, defended the Sunna and revived the way of Ahl al-Sunna, which the majority followed.

Together with his students he founded a fiqh academy that produced very fine and valuable ideas. In this fiqh academy all problems were assessed and discussed, and in the end they were decided in such a way as to become rulings.

To write the life of such an exalted personality has been a great honour for us. The one who brought about this fine thing was our esteemed businessman Mr Ayhan Yasan, who stands out with his efforts as a Muslim. I congratulate and commend him for this initiative. This work came into being in the form of a television series of 13 episodes. Although part of the script was written, the series could not be filmed because of certain difficulties. Even so, the attempt to film a series on a valuable personality is conduct that deserves appreciation.

Our work, prepared in the form of a biography, consists of an introduction, three chapters and a conclusion.
A bibliography has also been added at the end.
How happy we are if we have been able to open the door, even a little, for those who are interested…

Istanbul, 22.12.2024 Mehmet Çelen
