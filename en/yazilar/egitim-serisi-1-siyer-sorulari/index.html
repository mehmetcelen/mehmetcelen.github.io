Education Series 1: Questions on the Life of the Prophet

November 20, 2025About 9 min read

0:00

Read by an artificial voice. Arabic passages and footnotes are not read.

Translated from the Turkish original.Read the original

The questions on this page are assessment questions prepared to lead students to think during or at the end of a lesson on the life of the Prophet (sira). They are questions that an educator asks in order to open a discussion in class, to deepen the subjects and to measure the skill of applying knowledge of the sira to daily life. Throughout the text you will see both questions focused on knowledge and questions that call for interpretation; the aim is not only to recall historical events but to be able to establish the link between these events and the present day.

QUESTIONS ON THE LIFE OF THE PROPHET

Date: 17.09.2022 – Mehmet Çelen

1- What is "Hilf al-Fudul"? After the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) became a prophet, he said of "Hilf al-Fudul": "If there were such a pact now, I would join it." How can we draw a lesson from this hadith today?

2- Before prophethood came to Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him), he had been given the title "Muhammad al-Amin" (Muhammad the Trustworthy). How can you draw a lesson from this expression?

3- Why did Almighty Allah send as a prophet a human being who walks about in the bazaar and the market, who buys and sells, walks, loves, is loved, laughs, weeps, grieves, is happy, falls into difficulty, marries and has children? What does this make you think?

4- Why is Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him) counted as the "Seal of the Prophets" (Khatam al-Anbiya)?

5- The most important qualities and attributes of the prophets are truthfulness (sidq), trustworthiness (amana), freedom from sin ('isma), keen intelligence (fatana) and conveying the message (tabligh). How can you apply the attribute of trustworthiness to the present day?

6- Discuss whether the statement "The Prophet came as a postman, delivered his message and fulfilled his duty. Apart from that he has no function at all" is right or wrong. What do these words make you think today?

7- Why did the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) prefer the method of the secret call in the first years of prophethood (610-613 CE)?

8- What is the Hijra? What does the Hijra remind you of?

9- What do the concepts "Ansar" and "Muhajir" remind you of? Explain.

10- The great commander Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him) acted strategically in all the battles he fought. How can we apply this conduct of the Prophet to the present day?

11- The Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) used "Dar al-Arqam" as a centre in his activities of calling and guidance. What were his aims in using this place as a centre?

12- The Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) prayed at the Kaaba, where there were 360 idols. The Prophet, who was against the idols, performed his acts of worship in this way. How can we draw a lesson from this today?

13- After the year 613 CE the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) began to call openly to the religion of Islam. How did he put the open call to Islam into practice?

14- How did the polytheists of Mecca perceive prophethood?

15- What were the insults and slanders that the polytheists of Mecca directed at the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him), and why did this happen?

16- In the Battle of Uhud, fought in the year 625 CE, the Muslims suffered a defeat after the first stages and had heavy losses. How do we apply to the present day the fact that the 50 archers placed on the hill of Uhud left their positions?

17. How do we apply to the present day the noble hadith of our Prophet: "A Muslim may do anything, but he does not lie"?

18- In Mecca the polytheists of Mecca applied policies of violence, persecution and torture in order to make the Muslims give up the religion of Islam. Separate policies were applied to the free, to the merchants and to the slaves. What is the counterpart of this today, and what kind of measures should be taken?

19- Abu Bakr al-Siddiq (may Allah be pleased with him) used to buy Muslim slaves, even at an excessive price, and set them free. How can we establish the link between this event and the present day?

20- Where did the Muslims emigrate for the first time in the years 614 and 615 CE? What are the reasons for and the wisdom of this emigration?

21- In the years 617-620 CE the polytheists of Mecca imposed an embargo on the Muslims. This embargo was a commercial and social embargo. The Muslims suffered great hardships, and at the same time they showed great patience. How can we draw a lesson from this event today?

22- What is the protection system (himaya) of the Meccan administration? Both Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him) and some of the Companions benefited from this system of the polytheists. How do we apply this to the present day?

23– What happened in the Year of Sorrow (Sanat al-Huzn), and which great event took place after this year of sorrow? Explain.

24- What is the event of the Isra and the Mi'raj? Explain. Where are al-Masjid al-Haram and al-Masjid al-Aqsa, and who was the first builder of these mosques?

25- The Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) often visited the fairs and the tribes; what did he ask of them?

a. He calls to Allah and to His oneness.

b. He calls to the fundamentals of Islam.

c. He asks for support and help at a level that will let him carry out his duty as a messenger with ease.

d. He asks for a protection that will give him shelter.

26- What is the pledge (bay'a)? How many pledges took place, and where? What did the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) essentially ask for there?

"As for my condition and my request for myself: that you give shelter to me and my Companions, that you help me and my Companions, and that you defend and protect us from that from which you defend and protect yourselves.

Let me make a pledge with you that you will defend and protect me from that from which you defend and protect your women and your children!"

27- Of whom were the inhabitants of Medina made up? What did the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) want to achieve with the "Charter of Medina"? Write its aims.

28- After emigrating to Medina, the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) built the Mosque of the Prophet (al-Masjid al-Nabawi) as his first task. Which functions were carried out in this mosque?

  1. The centre of worship
  2. Centres of aid
  3. Schools and educational activities
  4. The centre of political activities
  5. The centre of the treasury and finance
  6. Military activities
  7. Judicial and legal activities
  8. Sport and game activities

29- After emigrating to Medina, the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) carried out a pact of brotherhood between the two communities. What were the aims of this pact?

30- What are the principles of the law of war that Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him) set out as a commander prophet?

a. To remain faithful to treaties

b. Not to kill civilians

c. Not to commit massacre and genocide

d. Not to mutilate the dead (muthla)

e. Not to kill hostages

f. Not to kill envoys

g. Not to molest women and children

h. Not to torture

i. To treat prisoners well

k. Not to harm nature

l. Not to harm settlements

31- Close to his death, Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him) appointed someone as young as Usama ibn Zayd (may Allah be pleased with him) as commander of the army prepared against Byzantium. What were the aims of this?

1- Usama ibn Zayd was brought to the central command on that day as the son of a freed slave. Thus the Prophet took a social step that would tear down the mentality of slavery.

2- By appointing as a high-ranking commander someone who was not from tribes such as Mudar, Quraysh and Umayya of that period, which put ethnic and tribal partisanship first, he wanted to tear down this mentality.

4- His appointing a young man in his twenties as commander taught that the way should be opened for the young, and that if a person is competent, age should not be looked at.

5- By placing great scholars and leading personalities such as Abu Bakr (may Allah be pleased with him) and Umar (may Allah be pleased with him) under the command of this young commander, he instilled the idea that Muslims of every age and status must work together and obey those in charge.

32- What happened as a result of the Peace Treaty of Hudaybiyya, made in February of the year 628 CE?

  1. The great conquest took place,
  2. International activities of the call increased,
  3. The Prophet's quality as head of state came to the fore,
  4. The Mosque of Dirar was demolished,
  5. Relations with the hypocrites were put in order,
  6. A divine ultimatum was given to the polytheists of Mecca.

33- How many children did Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him) have, and as the father of many children, what kind of father was he?

He had 7 children.

"He used to attend to the affairs of his family."

He married off all four of his daughters and, as a father, fulfilled whatever marriage requires.

He provided for both his wives and his children and did not leave them in need of anyone.

He took an interest in the upbringing, the manners and the education of his children and was the means of their becoming good Muslims.

He also took an interest in the health of his children and ensured that they grew up sound and strong.

34- What kind of person was Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him) as a husband and head of a family?

He was very compassionate towards his family.

He lightened the burden of the house and helped his wives.

He observed justice among his wives.

He did not leave his wives in need of anyone and met all the needs of the house.

He treated his wives with tolerance and won the heart of each one of them separately.

As a husband, he himself lived in his life as an example how to get on well with one's wives and treat them well.

He said: "The best of you are those who are best to their women."
