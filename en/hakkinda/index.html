Who is Mehmet Çelen?

Dr. Mehmet Çelen is a researcher, author, translator and educator working in the field of Islamic law (fiqh and usul al-fiqh). He is a sworn translator of Arabic, English and Ottoman Turkish, and has written and translated books, articles and conference papers.

Birth and early education

He was born in Malatya on 28 November 1961.
He began his first education at the ages of 4 and 5 in his father's Qur'an course, where he took lessons in Qur'an recitation, morals, creed and tajwid. The teachers of this course were his late father and mother.
After finishing primary school in 1971, he took private lessons in Arabic, hadith, fiqh and tafsir from two esteemed scholars of Malatya, Ustadh Hacı Mehmet Alptekin and Ustadh Mehmet Said Ertürk. This education continued until he graduated from university.
He graduated from Kubilay Middle School in 1974.
He graduated from Malatya Imam Hatip High School in 1978 with a good grade.

After graduation

  • He served as an imam and preacher in Kayseri for 4 months.
  • 1983 He graduated with a good grade from the Department of Kalam and Islamic Philosophy of the Faculty of Theology at Ondokuz Mayıs University in Samsun.
  • 1983 He taught Arabic, tafsir, fiqh and psychology at Malatya Yeşilyurt Imam Hatip High School.
  • 1983-1984 In this school year he worked as a teacher of Religious Culture and Ethics at Gölcük Middle School in Kocaeli.
  • 1984-1986 He did his military service in Istanbul as a reserve officer.
  • 1986-1988 He worked as a translator and interpreter in Istanbul.
  • 1988-1994 He worked as a sworn translator in Malatya and was engaged in trade.
  • 1993-1994 In Malatya he produced and presented a tafsir programme on Ufuk TV, a fiqh programme on Sena TV, and a tafsir programme and a programme called "The World of Politics" on Radyo Nida.
  • 1995-1999 He worked as a sworn translator in Istanbul and was engaged in freelance work.
  • 1999-2000 In this school year he was a teacher of Religious Culture and Ethics at Bilim College in Yenibosna, Istanbul.
  • 1999 He completed his master's degree with a good grade in the Department of Political History and International Relations of the Institute of Middle East Studies at Marmara University in Istanbul.
  • 2001 He completed his master's degree with a good grade in the Division of Islamic Law, Basic Islamic Sciences, of the Institute of Social Sciences at Istanbul University.
  • 2000-2004 In these school years he was an English teacher at the second level (middle school) of Nail Reşit Primary School in Bayrampaşa, Istanbul.
  • 2004-2007 In these school years he was the director and an English teacher at the ERBOY Foreign Language Course in Pendik, Istanbul.
  • 2007-2014 In these school years he was the director, the founding TÖMER manager and a teacher of Arabic and English at the International İNCİDEN Foreign Language School in Fatih, Istanbul. (For one year he taught Turkish to the Arab staff of TRT Arabic.)
  • 2007-2014 In these school years he taught fiqh, usul al-fiqh and family law at SEDAV AKADEMİ in Fatih, Istanbul.
  • 2011-2015 In these school years he taught family law at Restorasyon İstanbul Akademi in Fatih, Istanbul.
  • September 2011-September 2015 He completed his doctorate with the grade "very good" in the "Department of Law, Islamic Fiqh and Usul al-Fiqh" of the Institute of Islamic Sciences at the Da'wa University College (Külliyetü'd-Da've) in Beirut, the capital of the Republic of Lebanon. Subject of the thesis: "Shaykh al-Islam Molla Hüsrev: His Life, His Scholarly Personality and a Comparison of His Fiqh Views According to the Four Islamic Schools of Law" (Comparative Law).
  • 2014-2017 Research, translation and private lessons (fiqh, usul al-fiqh, Turkish lessons for Syrian Arab teachers).
  • 2017-2026 He continues his educational and teaching activities at the Maarif Foundation.

Platforms and boards:

  • He took part in the Prayer Volunteers Platform (Namaz Gönüllüleri Platformu); he gave more than 30 lectures and sermons on "Revival through Prayer" and took part in panels.
  • He took on active duties in the Halal Product Platform; he gave more than 10 lectures on halal food and attended the meetings of the Halal Food Scholarly Board.
  • As a member and the coordinator of the Fiqh Board, he drew up the "Halal Standards of Turkey" together with the Fiqh Board in May 2009.
