A Conversation on the Book "Yusuf al-Qaradawi's Understanding of Fiqh and His Contribution to Islamic Thought"

About 3 min read

Translated from the Turkish original.Read the original

Esad Eseoğlu: My esteemed teacher, how did such a fine piece of work come into being?

Dr. Mehmet Çelen: The story of how this work came about is as follows. My dear friend Assoc. Prof. Dr. Ramazan Yıldırım was told that a volume was being prepared for the esteemed master Prof. Dr. Yusuf al-Qaradawi, and since he was very busy he said that I could take on such a task. I said that I was available, and so we began the work.

Esad Eseoğlu: What was the nature of this work?

Dr. Mehmet Çelen: In Egypt a number of Muslim intellectuals have begun to produce books under a title which in Arabic is "Takrim al-'ulama' wa'l-mufakkirin wa'l-muthaqqafin", "Honouring the scholars, the thinkers and the intellectuals". This is a work that continues that series. That is, articles written about a man of learning by those who know him are collected, a symposium is held, papers are presented, and afterwards the published book is distributed to those who attend the symposium. In fact work of this kind ought to be done, in a fully developed form, for all the scholars and thinkers of Turkey as well. Our Ministry of Culture could carry this out.

Esad Eseoğlu: It is truly a very fine piece of work. And was such a symposium held for the master Prof. Dr. Yusuf al-Qaradawi?

Dr. Mehmet Çelen: Of course it was, and in Istanbul at that. It was held on 9 and 10 December 2016 at the Ali Emiri Cultural Centre in Fatih. I too was going to present a paper in Arabic, but I could not attend because of my illness.

Esad Eseoğlu: I believe three authors from Turkey took part. How did this come about? Could there not have been more?

Dr. Mehmet Çelen: Of course there could have been more. I passed this project on to five professors; they said that because they were so busy they would not be able to write such an article, and gave their excuses. I then put the proposal to Asst. Prof. Dr. Adnan Memduhoğlu, head of the department of Islamic law at the Faculty of Theology of Siirt University, and also to the researcher Hikmet Akpur. They kindly accepted. I too wrote an article, and so three contributions came from Turkey.

Esad Eseoğlu: What is the Arabic name of the published book?

Dr. Mehmet Çelen: The name of the book in Turkish is this: "The Esteemed Shaykh Yusuf al-Qaradawi, the Imam of Renewal and Moderation". It was published in two volumes, in the form of articles honouring him on the occasion of his reaching the age of 90 and treating his efforts in fiqh and his intellectual views. It was published by the Association of the Centre for the Study, Research and Teaching of Civilisation (Media-Mer).

Esad Eseoğlu: From whom did the idea come of bringing the articles together and publishing them in Turkish?

Dr. Mehmet Çelen: This idea came from me. I proposed it to my friends, and they too thought it suitable. I then proposed it to Nida Yayınları, which publishes all the books of Prof. Dr. Yusuf al-Qaradawi. Its editorial board kindly approved its publication, and so the three articles came out in the form of a small book.

Esad Eseoğlu: May your hands and your heart be blessed; it has turned out a very fine book.

Dr. Mehmet Çelen: I thank you very much for having studied such a book.
