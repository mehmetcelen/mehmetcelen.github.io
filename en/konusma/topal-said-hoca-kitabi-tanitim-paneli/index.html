Panel Presenting the Book "Topal Said Hoca"

About 1 min read

Translated from the Turkish original.Read the original

Malatya Metropolitan Municipality announced that a panel would be held on Saturday 24 September 2016 at 19.30, in the conference hall of the Art Gallery, to present the book "Topal Said Hoca" prepared by Mehmet Çelen.

According to the announcement, the speakers of the panel were Dr. Mehmet Çelen, Ahmet Ertürk, the son of Said Hoca, the researcher and writer Süleyman Aslantaş and the poet Metin Önal Mengüşoğlu.

Source: Büyükşehir’den Topal Said Hoca Paneli (İHA, 22 September 2016)
