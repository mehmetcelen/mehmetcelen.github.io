Mehmet Çelen

Dr. Mehmet Çelen is a researcher, author, translator and educator working in the field of Islamic law (fiqh and usul al-fiqh).

He is a sworn translator of Arabic, English and Ottoman Turkish, and has written and translated books, articles and conference papers.
