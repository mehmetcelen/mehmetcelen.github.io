Privacy

Translated from the Turkish original.Read the original

You do not need to register or give any information about yourself to read this site.

Cookies

This site does not use cookies. For this reason it does not show a cookie consent window either.

Visitor counting and advertising

The site has no counting tool that follows visitors, no advertising and no social media tracking code.

The only information kept in your browser

If you choose the light or the dark theme, this choice is kept only in your own browser. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Fonts and images

Fonts and images are loaded from this site itself. When you open a page, your browser does not connect to the server of another company.

Videos

The videos are on YouTube. This site shows only the cover image of the video, which is kept here. When you click the image, the video opens on YouTube in a new tab. From that moment on, YouTube's own privacy rules apply.

Listening to a text

The "Listen" button reads the text aloud with the voice of your own device. The text is not sent to another service for this.

Contact form

If you fill in the form on the Contact page, your name, your email address and your message are forwarded to Mehmet Çelen as an email. The forwarding is done by a form service called Web3Forms; the servers of the service are in the United States of America. According to its own statement, the service does not store the messages that are sent, it only forwards them. Your details are used only to reply to you and are not shared with anyone else. As long as you do not use the form, no information goes to this service, and the form does not use cookies.

Hosting of the site

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with every website, the hosting service may record your IP address for security purposes when you open a page. These records are kept by GitHub.

For your questions

You can send your questions about privacy from the Contact page.
